Masai needed to collect income-share agreements, codes of conduct, and other student documents. Physical paperwork made the process slow and expensive to coordinate.

MY ROLE & TEAM

Product design, working with a product manager, two backend engineers, two frontend engineers, and QA on an automated document workflow.
